# Documentation Guidelines

These guidelines define how projects should be documented to ensure they are **clear, reproducible, and easy to navigate**.

---

* [Documentation Structure](#documentation-structure)
* [Visuals & Diagrams](#visuals--diagrams)

## Documentation Structure

Each project should include a **well-structured document** (in `README.md` or inside a `docs/` folder) with the following sections:

---

### 1. Abstract

* Brief overview of the project
* What it does and why it exists

---

### 2. Problem Statement

* What problem is being solved?
* Why is it relevant?

---

### 3. Objectives

* Clear goals of the project
* What you aimed to achieve

---

### 4. Mechanical Design *(if applicable)*

* Physical structure and components
* Design decisions and reasoning

---

### 5. Electronics Design *(if applicable)*

* Circuit overview
* Components used
* Explanation of connections

---

### 6. Software & Logic *(if applicable)*

* Code structure and workflow
* Algorithms or logic used
* Key implementation details

---

### 7. Challenges Faced

* Problems encountered during development
* How they were resolved

---

### 8. Potential Improvements

* What could be improved or extended
* Future ideas

---

### 9. Conclusion

* Final outcome
* Key takeaways

---

## Visuals & Diagrams

* Include **at least 2–3 relevant visuals**
* Prefer:

  * Circuit schematics
  * System diagrams / flowcharts
  * Final project images

### Guidelines

* Keep visuals **clear and labeled**
* Avoid low-quality or cluttered images
* Use diagrams where text becomes hard to follow

---

## Philosophy

* Documentation should help others:

  * Understand the project
  * Recreate it with minimal effort
